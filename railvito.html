Railvito: Kids Train Games 3D

Privacy Policy · Version 1.7.0 · Updated 27 November 2026
Android package: com.mhmmad.railvito

English (UK)

Railvito is an offline railway puzzle game intended for children aged 4–10. This version contains no advertising SDKs, accounts, analytics, chat, in-app purchases or collection or transmission of personal data. It does not request Internet access, location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

Mission progress, earned stars, train selection, language, sound, display and control preferences are saved locally on the device. These records are not sent to the developer or to a server. Parents can delete them using Android Settings → Apps → Railvito → Clear data, or by uninstalling the game.

The game includes a local parental gate. A parent may choose to open the privacy policy in the device's browser; that browser and website are subject to their own privacy practices. The game does not transmit its saved progress when opening the policy. If the game’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.

Developer: Mohammad Suleiman, Jordan. Privacy enquiries: mhmmad444410aa@gmail.com.

Português (Brasil) — Política de Privacidade

Railvito é um jogo offline de quebra-cabeças ferroviários destinado a crianças de 4 a 10 anos. Esta versão não contém SDKs de publicidade, contas, análises, bate-papo, compras no aplicativo nem coleta ou transmissão de dados pessoais. O jogo não transmite o progresso salvo ao abrir a política. Esta política será atualizada antes de mudanças nas práticas de tratamento de dados.

Version 1.8.0 — Advertising privacy addendum

Version 1.7.0 remains ad-free as described above. Version 1.8.0 includes the Petal Ads SDK (Huawei/Aspiegel). The game itself remains playable without internet and without consent to advertising. Before requesting advertising, the game requests consent from a parent or guardian in the parents area and stores the choice and date locally. The adult may withdraw it there at any time. Requests are configured for child-directed and under-age audiences, contextual/non-personalised ads only and content rating W. The game requests internet and network state permissions, not location, camera, contacts or microphone access. The ad SDK may process technical connection information and ad events necessary to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure performance under its own privacy terms; the child-directed setting restricts personal-data collection and third-party ad requests according to Huawei's policy. We do not send game progress or account data to Petal Ads. See Petal Ads Privacy and Data Security Policies for provider, processing, retention and rights details. Ads may appear in a separate bottom area during gameplay and on the main menu, and between completed missions. You can use the contact address above to exercise privacy rights.

Versão 1.8.0 — Adendo de privacidade publicitária

A versão 1.7.0 continua sem anúncios, como descrito acima. A versão 1.8.0 inclui o SDK Petal Ads (Huawei/Aspiegel). O jogo continua disponível offline e sem consentimento publicitário. Anúncios podem aparecer em área separada na parte inferior da tela inicial e durante o jogo, e entre missões concluídas. Use o e-mail acima para exercer direitos de privacidade.
